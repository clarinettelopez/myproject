# Mybudget

* A simple budgeting app for managing personal finances.
* Single-page HTML 5 app

## Features

* Enter regular and one-off income
* Enter regular and one-off expenses
* Balance tracking
* Prediction of future balances based on current income and expenses
* Tables view
* Graph view

## Design

* Simple and intuitive user interface
* Colors : white, dark green, light green, and black
* Responsive design
